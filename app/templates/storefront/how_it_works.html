The consumer journey

3 clicks to checkout
  • 1
    Build your basket
    One basket, one store. We show a live progress bar to the {{ NB.min_order_value|naira }} floor and suggest add-ons that close the gap to your {{ NB.target_aov|naira }} target basket.
  • 2
    Pay into escrow
    Bank transfer, debit card or USSD. Funds are held, not handed over. The platform fee is calculated server-side — never trusted from the browser.
  • 3
    Partner picks in {{ NB.merchant_sla_minutes }} minutes
    The store's OMS opens a countdown the second the payment settles. Blow the window and the order escalates with an SLA breach recorded against the store's rating.
  • 4
    Courier is dispatched automatically
    Marking the order packed fires the 3PL bridge inside the {{ NB.delivery_radius_km }} km radius. The rider payload deliberately carries no PIN and no exact street number.
  • 5
    Hand over your PIN
    Read the 4-digit code to the courier when your basket is in your hands. That single act releases the merchant payout and the rider fee.

Why the {{ NB.min_order_value|naira }} floor exists

Scheduled household baskets only work when delivery overhead is spread across a real basket. The floor protects the merchant's margin, keeps the {{ NB.convenience_fee|naira }} platform fee sane, and lets the courier quote a fair fare — so nobody has to cut corners on your order.

Subscriptions

A monthly pass spreads its price across the orders you place, which is why subscribers see a recognised revenue allocation on every checkout. Fewer, bigger, predictable baskets — better for everyone in the chain.
